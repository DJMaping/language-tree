# History draft (Phase 5)

Proposals for DJ to approve before any of it reaches the chart or the engine. Built 2026-10-13 by `scripts/build-history-draft.js`; the same proposals as data in `data/history-draft.json`.

**In short:** 37 dated events from the wiki that touch languages, 5 wars, and 116 colonial ties. They give **25 contact arrows** that can be drawn and fed to the engine today, **10 date conflicts** between the tree and the wiki, and **6 languages** the wiki names that the chart does not have.

## Questions for DJ

1. Colonial rule: the ties give the year of independence but not when rule began. Should colonial contact start a fixed span before independence (say 150 years), or will you give start dates?
2. Conquest outcomes: the draft proposes heavy, light, shift or little trace for each conquest (column "proposed"). Which do you want changed?
3. Languages named in history but not in the chart (list below): add them as extinct languages (Unwosian and Carth as isolates, the others as members of their families), or leave them as names only?
4. Date conflicts (list below): for each, should the tree move to the wiki date (the default) or is the wiki wrong?
5. Lastnu: when, where and by whom was it constructed, and when did it gain native speakers? (Drawn at a placeholder 1700.)
6. Writing: the wiki dates Dahen bone script to c. 1400 BFA and its standardisation to 207 BFA. Where and when did the other families first write?
7. Atirhan Dahen: Emaran plantation labourers speaking Dahen (Emaran as lexifier), or a Dahen community restructured under Emaran rule (Dahen as lexifier)?
8. The blank in Estijan: "the Uvei kingdom adopting ____ from the Yethran Empire" in 842. What did they adopt?
